## Collagen Reflectance Analyzer 1.1

### Downloads
Pick the zip for your computer. It is attached below a few minutes after this release appears.
- **CollagenAnalyzer-mac-apple-silicon.zip**: Macs with M1, M2, M3 or M4 chips
- **CollagenAnalyzer-mac-intel.zip**: older Intel Macs
- **CollagenAnalyzer-windows.zip**: Windows 10 and 11

Unzip the file and open the app. On a Mac the first time, right-click the app → **Open**, because the app is not signed. The user manual (PDF) is inside the zip.

### New in 1.1
**Controls and statistics**
- **Sham controls:** put `sham` in a file name to mark a sham gel (same handling, no field), and `noNC` to mark a gel without nanocages. Every gel is sorted into a group: NC + AMF, AMF only, sham, or sham without NCs.
- **Control-corrected change:** each AMF change, minus the change of the sham gels from the same day.
- **Statistics with the gel as the replicate:**
  - For each group: mean, 95 % CI, and paired tests of the change.
  - Between groups: Welch and Mann–Whitney tests, Hedges' g and Holm-adjusted p-values. *NC + AMF vs sham* is the field-effect test.
  - These go into three new sheets: `changes`, `statistics` and `arm comparisons`.
- **Registration and change maps:** the app lines up each After image with its Before image and maps where the alignment, fiber density and pore size changed (`change_map_….png`, `…_tile_changes.csv`).
- **Experiment Planner:** subtracts the sham change by default. It also has a nanocage-physics box (Brownian and Néel relaxation, phase lag, torque).

**New look**
- A cleaner design. **New analysis** walks you through Files → Settings → Outputs → Run → Results.
- The Files step has a **Ready to run** panel. It warns about a gel without a Before file, a day without sham gels, or files the quality check flagged.
- **Night mode** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Saved figures stay white.

**Graphs**
- A **Style** panel sets fonts and sizes, legend and label positions, bar width and opacity, individual data points, error bars (now also 95 % CI), **trend lines** (linear, log-x, mean, with R² and a 95 % band), axes and colours. It includes a colour-blind-safe palette.
- Ready-made styles (journal figure, slides, greyscale), and you can save your own.
- **Drag any label with the mouse.** Journal and slide figure sizes are shown on screen at their real size.

**Other**
- Automatic tests, including synthetic fiber networks with known alignment.
- MIT license, a citation file ("Cite this repository"), and third-party notices.
- Fixes: Qt background errors on two pages; newly loaded results were unticked; y labels overlapped when there were several rows of panels.
